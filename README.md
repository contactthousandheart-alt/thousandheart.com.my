# Thousand Heart 长者关怀

Official website for **Thousand Heart 长者关怀 | Elder Care**.

## Brand Message

**有人定期探望，家人更安心。**  
**Regular Care Visits, Greater Peace of Mind.**

We provide scheduled home visits, companionship, basic wellness observation, care records, and post-visit family updates for seniors and families living apart.

## Our Care

- 定期探访 | Regular Home Visits
- 陪伴关怀 15–20 分钟 | Companionship 15–20 mins
- 基本健康观察 | Basic Wellness Observation
  - 血压、血氧、体重
  - Blood Pressure, Blood Oxygen and Weight
- 关怀记录 | Care Records
- 探访后家属更新 | Post-visit Family Updates

## Care Plans

## Suitable For

- Seniors living alone
- Seniors with limited mobility
- Families living apart
- Families who would like someone to visit and spend time with their elderly parents regularly

## Important Notice

This is a non-medical elder care service.

We do not provide:

- Diagnosis
- Treatment
- Prescriptions
- Injections
- Wound care
- Emergency response

Basic wellness observation is carried out only with the senior’s consent and when circumstances allow.
