About Us

为什么顾客会选择我们

我们理解很多家庭最大的担心，是爸妈一个人在家却没有人定期留意。我们的服务重点不是复杂医疗，而是把基础监测、记录、家属汇报与温和关怀做好，让长者在熟悉的家中也能获得更安心的关注。

We understand that many families worry when elderly parents live alone. Our focus is not complex medical treatment, but making basic monitoring, records, family updates, and caring support more reliable and reassuring.

FAQ

常见问题 / Frequently Asked Questions

你们是不是诊所或护士服务？ / Are you a clinic or nursing service?

我们主要提供上门基础健康监测、记录、家属同步与关怀服务，不提供医疗诊断、处方或治疗。

We mainly provide home basic health monitoring, records, family updates, and caring support. We do not provide diagnosis, prescriptions, or treatment.

适合哪些家庭？ / Who is this suitable for?

适合独居老人、行动不便长者、慢性病长者，以及子女不在身边、希望有人定期上门关注的家庭。

Suitable for elderly living alone, seniors with limited mobility, seniors needing regular monitoring, and families whose children are living apart.

每次服务后会通知家属吗？ / Will family members receive updates after each visit?

可以。每次服务后可按安排将监测结果与简短情况同步给家属，让家人更安心。

Yes. After each visit, monitoring results and a short update can be sent to family members for better peace of mind.
